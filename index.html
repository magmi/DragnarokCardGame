Welcome, Warrior

You play as Krystel. Defeat every enemy on your path and survive to claim victory.

Choose Your Battle

Select an enemy on the map and press Begin Battle. Each encounter grows more dangerous.

Play Your Cards

Each turn, you draw a hand of cards. Click a card to play it and combine attacks, defense and effects.

Manage Energy

Every card costs Energy. You begin each turn with 3 Energy Orbs, so spend them wisely and end your turn when ready.

Read Enemy Intent

Enemies reveal their next action above their heads. Use this information to prepare.

Use Block

Your block absorbs incoming damage and resets each turn.

Watch Status Effects

Enemy also has Block. Use Burn and Vulnerable status - they can tip the scales.

Grow Stronger

Defeat is not the end. Earn EXP from fallen enemies and return stronger for the next battle. Good Luck!

How to Play

Play cards at the bottom of the screen to attack enemies, defend, and survive.

  • Click a card to play it if you have enough energy.
  • End your turn and wait for the enemy to act.
  • Defeat the opponent before your HP reaches 0.

Energy

You begin each turn with 3 Energy Orbs. Spend them to play cards. Each card's cost is displayed at the bottom of the card.

Status Effects

shield Block

Absorbs incoming damage before HP is lost. Resets to 0 at the start of each of your turns.

mode_heat Burn

Deals damage equal to burn stacks. Decreases by 1 each turn.

heart_minus Vulnerable

Takes 25% more damage from attacks. Decreases by 1 each turn.
