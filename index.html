How to Play

Use your energy to play cards. Attack the Drake, gain Block, and survive its next move.

  • Click a card to play it if you have enough energy.
  • End your turn to let the enemy execute its intent.
  • Block resets after your turn ends.
  • Defeat the enemy before your HP reaches 0.
