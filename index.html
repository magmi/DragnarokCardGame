Welcome, Warrior

You play as Krystel. Defeat every enemy on your path and survive to claim victory.

Choose Your Battle

Select an enemy on the map and press Begin Battle. Each encounter grows more dangerous.

Play Your Cards

Each turn, you draw a hand of cards. Click a card to play it and combine attacks, defense and effects.

Manage Energy

Every card costs Energy. You begin each turn with 3 Energy Orbs, so spend them wisely and end your turn when ready.

Read Enemy Intent

Enemies reveal their next action above their heads. Use this information to prepare.

Use Block

Your block absorbs incoming damage and resets each turn.

Watch Status Effects

Enemy also has Block. Use Burn and Vulnerable status - they can tip the scales.

Grow Stronger

Defeat is not the end. Earn EXP from fallen enemies and return stronger for the next battle. Good Luck!
