How to Play

Play cards at the bottom of the screen to attack enemies, defend, and survive.

  • Click a card to play it if you have enough energy.
  • End your turn and wait for the enemy to act.
  • Defeat the opponent before your HP reaches 0.

Energy

You begin each turn with 3 Energy Orbs. Spend them to play cards. Each card's cost is displayed at the bottom of the card.

Status Effects

shield Block

Absorbs incoming damage before HP is lost. Resets to 0 at the start of each of your turns.

mode_heat Burn

Deals damage equal to burn stacks. Decreases by 1 each turn.

heart_minus Vulnerable

Takes 25% more damage from attacks. Decreases by 1 each turn.
